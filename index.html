ℹ️ Transparent Priority Scoring Formula

Mathematical Objective Function:
Priority Score = 0.50 × V_norm + 0.35 × G_norm + 0.15 × U_norm

To guarantee transparency and prevent arbitrary black-box budgeting, Awaaz Setu weighs three objective, verifiable dimensions for every district sector:

  • 1. Normalized Request Volume (50% Weight):
    Direct citizen participation volume. Raw count of verified, deduplicated citizen voice signals normalized across national percentiles (\(V_{norm} \in [0, 100]\)).
  • 2. Normalized Infrastructure Deficit (35% Weight):
    Empirical infrastructure readiness index (0–100, where 0 = severe collapse and 100 = full saturation). Gap is \(G = 100 - \text{InfraIndex}\).
  • 3. Normalized Under-Investment (15% Weight):
    Fiscal gap between benchmark capital needed based on population & deficit vs. currently planned state budget allocation (\(U_{norm} \in [0, 100]\)).
Auditable Governance: Every ranked recommendation links directly back to raw cluster transcripts, geographic boundary coordinates, and public department allocations.
